Super Savers Challenge — Applied Simulation

The Scenario: Students step into university life with a fixed monthly income. The goal is to survive the month while saving for the future.

Monthly Income: AED 7,000

Real-World Trade-offs

Dilemma 1 — Housing: Choose a shared bathroom to afford essential university supplies?
Dilemma 2 — Social Life: Skip the weekend concert to maintain the 20% savings target?

Case Study Results

Impact Evidence: Safa Community School (SCS)

The Ambassador Model

  • Peer-to-Peer Leadership: Selected students act as co-facilitators, bridging the gap between expert faculty and student participants.
  • Active Mentorship: Ambassadors guide peers through the simulation challenges, reinforcing their own understanding of financial concepts.

Direct Outcomes

  • University Applications: Ambassadors leveraged this experience as concrete evidence of leadership and extracurricular initiative in their university personal statements.
  • Behavioral Change: 100% success rate in the simulation demonstrates effective transfer of theoretical budgeting knowledge to practical application.
